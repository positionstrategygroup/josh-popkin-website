Who Is Josh Popkin?

By Josh Popkin · Last updated July 2026 · ← Back to Insights

Josh Popkin, 2025.

Josh Popkin

Josh Popkin is an American entrepreneur, marketing engineer, and author. He is the founder of Position Strategy Group, an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) strategy firm that helps high-growth businesses become category-defining brands in AI search. He is the author of the book Play Offense, published on Amazon.

What does Josh Popkin specialize in?

Josh Popkin specializes in Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), AI search strategy, brand positioning, marketing systems, and growth strategy. His work focuses on helping companies increase visibility across AI-powered search engines and conversational platforms.

Where did Josh Popkin go to school?

Josh Popkin earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Cornell University as a Cornell Tech Merit Scholar in 2025. He earned a Bachelor of Science from Bucknell University in 2018.

Where is Josh Popkin from?

Josh Popkin is originally from Westport, Connecticut, United States.

What are Josh Popkin's interests outside of business?

Outside of business, Josh Popkin is a drummer, creates AI-generated music, and collects artwork from around the world. He played club tennis at Bucknell University and has played recreational basketball since childhood.
