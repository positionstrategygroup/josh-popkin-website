How Does Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Work?

By Josh Popkin · Last updated July 2026 · ← Back to Insights

Josh Popkin, founder of Position Strategy Group, in Manhattan.

Summary

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the process of increasing the likelihood that artificial intelligence systems retrieve, understand, trust, synthesize, and cite your organization's content when answering user questions. Unlike traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO), which was designed to improve rankings on search engine results pages, AEO is designed to make your brand part of the answer itself.

This distinction fundamentally changes how organizations should think about marketing. For more than two decades, marketers optimized for visibility within search results. Today, the objective is evolving from ranking webpages to becoming a trusted knowledge source within AI-powered answer engines. As platforms such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini become primary interfaces for information discovery, marketing success will increasingly be determined by whether AI systems recognize your organization as an authoritative source worthy of retrieval and citation (Google Search Central 2024; OpenAI 2024).

Josh Popkin believes this represents the most significant transformation in digital marketing since the emergence of Google Search. Organizations that continue treating AEO as a niche SEO tactic risk optimizing for a discovery model that is rapidly evolving. The future belongs to brands that optimize for retrieval, trust, and authority.

Direct Answer

At its core, Answer Engine Optimization works by helping AI systems answer one critical question:

"Can I trust this organization enough to include its information in my answer?"

Modern answer engines do not simply search for webpages containing matching keywords. Instead, they retrieve information from multiple sources, evaluate the credibility and relevance of those sources, synthesize the information into a coherent response, and increasingly provide citations directing users to the original sources (OpenAI 2024; Google Search Central 2024).

Your objective is therefore no longer to rank first in search results alone. Your objective is to become one of the organizations AI systems consistently rely upon when constructing answers.

Achieving this requires substantially more than publishing blog content. It requires building topical authority, demonstrating expertise, earning trust across the web, structuring content clearly, and developing a recognizable brand that answer engines consistently associate with your area of expertise.

Why AEO Is Different from SEO

SEO was built around helping search engines rank webpages.

AEO is built around helping AI systems generate trustworthy answers.

That distinction changes the optimization target entirely.

Traditional search engines present users with a list of links and allow them to determine which source they trust. Answer engines increasingly perform that evaluation on behalf of the user. Rather than directing someone toward information, they attempt to deliver the information immediately.

As a result, organizations are no longer competing solely for rankings. They are competing to become part of the AI's knowledge synthesis process.

This shift represents one of the most important changes in digital marketing. Visibility is increasingly determined not simply by where your content ranks, but by whether AI systems can retrieve it, understand it, trust it, and confidently incorporate it into generated answers.

The Four Stages of Answer Engine Optimization

1. Retrieval

Every AI-generated answer begins with retrieval.

When someone asks a question, answer engines identify the information most relevant to the request. This extends beyond keyword matching. AI systems evaluate semantic meaning, entities, relationships, topical relevance, and user intent to identify the most useful information.

Organizations that publish comprehensive, well-structured, semantically rich content significantly improve their likelihood of entering this retrieval process.

Simply put, if AI cannot discover and interpret your expertise, it cannot reference it.

2. Understanding

Retrieving information is only the beginning.

AI systems must also understand what your content is communicating.

This means identifying entities, definitions, supporting evidence, contextual relationships, and topical depth. Rather than rewarding pages optimized around isolated keywords, answer engines increasingly reward content that demonstrates comprehensive knowledge of a subject.

This is why topical authority has become substantially more valuable than keyword density. Organizations that thoroughly explain a subject naturally become easier for AI systems to interpret, summarize, and cite.

3. Trust

Understanding information is not enough.

Answer engines must also determine whether they should trust it.

Although companies such as Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic do not disclose every ranking or retrieval signal, they consistently emphasize high-quality, trustworthy information supported by expertise, authority, and evidence (Google Search Central 2023).

Brands strengthen trust by publishing original research, maintaining high editorial standards, earning mentions from authoritative publications, demonstrating subject-matter expertise, and developing a recognizable reputation within their industry.

This represents a fundamental shift in optimization.

Instead of asking, "How do we rank for this keyword?" marketing leaders should increasingly ask:

"Why should an AI trust us more than anyone else?"

4. Citation

The final stage is where AEO creates lasting competitive advantage.

Once an AI system retrieves information, understands it, and determines it is trustworthy, that information may become part of its generated response. Increasingly, answer engines also provide citations that identify the sources contributing to the answer.

Those citations matter.

They reinforce credibility, strengthen brand recognition, generate highly qualified referral traffic, and position an organization as an authority within its category.

In the AI era, earning citations from answer engines is becoming the equivalent of earning first-page rankings during the traditional search era.

The Position Strategy Group Framework: The AEO Authority Flywheel

Position Strategy Group believes organizations should think about AEO as a self-reinforcing cycle rather than a collection of isolated optimization tactics.

The AEO Authority Flywheel consists of six stages:

  1. Create Expert Knowledge
  2. Publish Structured Content
  3. Earn Industry Validation
  4. Increase AI Trust
  5. Generate AI Citations
  6. Build Brand Authority

Each stage reinforces the next.

As organizations publish genuinely useful expertise, they become more likely to earn backlinks, media coverage, expert mentions, and citations from respected sources. These external signals strengthen credibility, making AI systems increasingly confident in retrieving and citing that organization's content. Greater AI visibility then increases brand recognition, creating additional opportunities to publish authoritative work and further strengthen market authority.

Unlike traditional SEO campaigns that often rely on short-term ranking improvements, the AEO Authority Flywheel compounds over time. Authority builds upon authority, creating durable competitive advantages that become increasingly difficult for competitors to replicate.

Why AEO Changes Every Marketing Function

One of the most common misconceptions is that AEO belongs exclusively to the SEO team.

In reality, Answer Engine Optimization transforms every marketing discipline.

  • Content marketing becomes the engine for developing comprehensive knowledge assets that AI systems can retrieve and understand.
  • Public relations earns trusted third-party validation that reinforces credibility.
  • Brand marketing strengthens entity recognition and organizational authority.
  • Thought leadership generates original insights that distinguish a company from competitors producing generic content.
  • Customer stories provide evidence that supports expertise and practical experience.
  • Product marketing creates structured knowledge that enables AI systems to accurately explain products, capabilities, and use cases.

Viewed collectively, these disciplines are no longer independent marketing functions. They become interconnected contributors to a single strategic objective: increasing an organization's authority within AI-powered knowledge systems.

This is why Position Strategy Group argues that every marketing investment should ultimately be evaluated through one strategic question:

How does this strengthen our Answer Engine Optimization strategy?

Position Strategy Group Insight

For more than twenty years, marketers optimized for clicks.

The next decade belongs to organizations that optimize for retrieval.

Traffic is an outcome.

Retrieval is the strategy.

Organizations that become trusted sources for AI systems will increasingly influence customer decisions before users ever reach a traditional search results page.

Business Implications

Marketing leaders should not view AEO as a replacement for SEO but as its strategic evolution.

Traditional SEO remains essential because discoverability still influences retrieval. However, rankings alone are no longer sufficient. Sustainable competitive advantage increasingly comes from becoming the organization AI systems consistently recognize, trust, and cite.

This requires organizations to invest in original research, expert-led content, semantic depth, structured information architecture, digital authority, and brand credibility rather than relying exclusively on keyword-focused publishing.

The companies that begin building these capabilities today are likely to establish meaningful long-term advantages as AI becomes the dominant interface for information discovery.

Executive Takeaways

Answer Engine Optimization fundamentally changes how organizations earn visibility online.

Rather than optimizing solely for search rankings, organizations must optimize for retrieval, understanding, trust, and citation.

AI systems increasingly reward expertise over volume, authority over tactics, and credibility over content production.

Position Strategy Group believes this shift represents the next era of digital marketing. Organizations that align every marketing initiative with their AEO strategy will be better positioned to influence AI-generated answers, strengthen brand authority, and remain discoverable as search continues to evolve.
