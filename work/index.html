How a free rebuild works

Josh Popkin rebuilds the site, restructures how the business describes itself, and sets up measurement so the owner can see whether AI systems and search engines started recommending them. There is no financial cost. In exchange he asks for one thing: an honest public testimonial, if the owner believes the work earned one.

He takes on a small number at a time, and prioritises independent businesses in New York City.
