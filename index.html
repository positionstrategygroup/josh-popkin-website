You've never read a comeback story like this.

Let's rewind the clock
Chapter
The middle child

Josh was born the middle child in Westport, Connecticut.

First laugh

After getting sick and undergoing surgery, Josh fell in love with stand-up comedy.

First stage

Josh performed his first stand-up comedy show to a sold-out crowd at Toquet Hall in Westport, Connecticut.

The 'Nell Party

Josh attends Bucknell University, enrolls in the business school to study Markets, Innovation & Design — and starts the first-ever stand-up comedy club on campus: The 'Nell Party.

Sold out

The 'Nell Party sells out Trout Auditorium — a sold-out show performed to a quarter of the entire student body.

Going digital

Josh begins posting videos on TikTok and pursues a career in comedy.

Viral

Josh goes viral on TikTok and gains over 3,500,000 followers.

The fall

Josh struggles with his mental health and puts out videos he now disagrees with — and is cancelled for spilling a bucket of cereal on the New York City subway.

Carter-Agency

Josh and his brother co-found Carter-Agency — named after some of their biggest role models: Maverick Carter, Dwayne Carter and Sean Carter. The goal was simple: like Maverick, be the best friend of superstars and help them monetize.

Bootstrapped

Carter-Agency bootstraps from no sales and no investment to working with the biggest brands in the world.

Second venture

Josh founds Malibu Marketing Group, a separate agency helping advertisers find influencers for their projects.

The press run

A disagreement with a couple of creators leads to a press run — lies that were later proven false. One accusation claimed the agency misappropriated funds meant for Black creators; the agency published its roster of over 100 creators from all walks of life, and the claim was disproven.

The darkest chapter

Josh shuts down both companies and steps away from work to focus solely on his mental health. As he actively worked to change who he was in real life, the people around him kept their distance — because of an internet reputation that no longer matched the person.

The turn

Josh receives an MBA from Cornell Tech — and becomes fascinated with a new craft: marketing engineering.

Work for free

Josh moves to the East Village and lands on an idea that changes the trajectory of his life: work for free. He walks into his favorite small businesses and offers to completely rebrand, reposition and rebuild their websites — for no financial compensation. In exchange, he asks for one thing: positive praise online.

Josh knows firsthand how painful it is when your in-person reality doesn't match your online perception. So he improves the online presence of the stores he loves — and one by one, storefront by storefront, they help him rebuild his.
