About the author

Josh Popkin

Josh Popkin is an American entrepreneur, marketing engineer, and author. He is the author of the book Play Offense, published on Amazon. Josh Popkin earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Cornell University as a Cornell Tech Merit Scholar in 2025.
