Biography

About Josh Popkin

Josh Popkin is a marketing engineer based in New York City. He rebuilds websites for independent businesses so that people and AI assistants can find, understand and recommend them.

Josh Popkin's work sits between marketing and engineering: website architecture, structured data, automation, and the measurement that shows whether any of it changed anything. The organising question is narrow and answerable — when someone asks about a business, in Google or in an AI assistant, what answer comes back?

He earned an MBA from Cornell Tech in 2025 and a business degree from Bucknell University in 2018. Before moving into marketing he spent seven years performing stand-up comedy and built an audience of more than 3.5 million followers online. Between 2020 and 2024 he co-founded and ran an influencer marketing agency that connected creators with brands including Nike, Amazon, Google, McDonald's, Visa and Meta.

He wrote the marketing book Play Offense, and releases music under his own name on Spotify and Apple Music.

Expertise

What Josh Popkin works on

Josh Popkin's practice covers four areas, and they are sequential rather than separate — measurement first, then structure, then content, then the systems that keep it running.

  • Answer engine optimization (AEO). Making a business legible to ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, and tracking how often those systems name it.
  • Search optimization. The conventional layer underneath: crawlability, structured data, sitemaps, indexing, page structure.
  • Website architecture. Rebuilding sites so that the information a customer needs is also the information a machine can quote.
  • Marketing automation. The workflows behind the work — publishing, measurement, reporting.

He measures AI visibility with Profound and Peec AI, and search performance with Google Search Console and IndexNow. His guide to answer engine optimization explains the method in full, including a measured baseline from a live client engagement.
